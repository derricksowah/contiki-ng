WIRESHARK / TSHARK VALIDATION
=============================

The live detector and mitigator operate inside Cooja.
Wireshark/tshark are used as independent packet-level
validation tools.

Representative seed:
101

Capture scenarios:
1. Baseline
2. Blackhole attack-only
3. Blackhole mitigated
4. Grayhole attack-only
5. Grayhole mitigated
6. Decreased Rank attack-only
7. Decreased Rank mitigated
8. DODAG Version attack-only
9. DODAG Version mitigated
10. DIS Flood attack-only
11. DIS Flood mitigated

Validate:
- UDP delivery behaviour
- ICMPv6/RPL control traffic
- DIS flood increase and suppression
- DIO/version behaviour
- routing behaviour before and after mitigation

Recommended tshark export fields:
frame.number
frame.time_relative
frame.len
ipv6.src
ipv6.dst
icmpv6.type
icmpv6.code
udp.srcport
udp.dstport
